# 🎨 CITY PAINTER

**Uncover the City. Feel the Calm. Paint the Night.**

Welcome to **City Painter** – a uniquely soothing and visually captivating ASMR-inspired game where your only tool is a _scraper_... and your canvas? The hidden beauty of iconic cities around the world.

## 🕹️ Gameplay

In **City Painter**, players gently scrape away the gray coating that hides breathtaking illustrations of world-famous landmarks – starting with the magical **night sky over Paris**.

- Use your **scraper** tool to reveal vibrant cityscapes piece by piece.
- Enjoy immersive **ASMR sound effects** as you scrape – think soft swishes, clicks, and crumbles.
- Relax to peaceful **background soundtracks** including:
  - ☔ Rainy days
  - 🔥 Cozy fires
  - ⚡ Occasional thunder
  - And even **cats meowing** in the distance...

## 🌈 Features

- ✨ Satisfying **scraping mechanics** that feel real.
- 🎧 ASMR-rich **audio experience** designed to calm and delight.
- 🎵 **Ambient music** and city-themed soundscapes.
- 🗼 Unlock famous **landmarks** like the Eiffel Tower, Big Ben, and more.
- 🌆 Beautiful, **hand-crafted illustrations** revealed as you progress.

## 💡 Why You'll Love It

If you're into **relaxing games**, enjoy **art**, or just need to de-stress while admiring some stunning visuals – **City Painter** is the perfect digital escape.

Whether you're playing for 5 minutes or 50, each scrape brings peace, color, and life to your screen.
